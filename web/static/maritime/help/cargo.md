## Two capacities, not one

A hold runs out of **space** or it runs out of **weight**, and which one bites first depends
on the cargo. A hold full of feathers is full long before she is heavy; a hold of iron is
down to her marks with the hold half empty.

That is what a stowage factor is, and it is why "how much can she carry" has no single
answer.

## What a full hold does to her

Four things, and none of them is a number in a menu:

- **She floats deeper.** Which means shoaler water is dangerous — see
  [soundings](soundings.md).
- **She is slower.**
- **She is slower to answer her helm.**
- **She is stiffer or tenderer** depending on where the weight is. Weight low is stiff and
  safe; weight high is tender, and a tender ship in a sea is a ship on her beam ends.

## What it is worth

Nothing here has an opinion about that. Prices, contracts, freight rates and who is buying
belong to the game this is installed in, because they collide with whatever economy it
already has. The islands each want one cargo and offer another, which is enough to make a
round worth running.
